Cedar Desk Fan — fictional product manual, revision A

Specifications
The Cedar Desk Fan has three speeds: 800, 1200, and 1600 RPM. Its supply must be USB-C 5 V, 3 A. It has no battery. Maximum power consumption is 12 W. The base diameter is 140 mm and the head tilts up to 35 degrees. Use indoors between 5 and 35 degrees Celsius.

Controls and timer
Tap the leaf button to cycle through low, medium, high, and off. Hold the leaf button for 2 seconds to enable a 45-minute shutoff timer; the blue timer light comes on. Hold it again for 2 seconds to cancel the timer. Unplugging the fan clears the timer setting.

Cleaning and placement
Disconnect power and wait until the blades stop before cleaning. Turn the front grille counterclockwise to remove it. Use a dry brush on the blades. Keep at least 15 cm of clearance behind the fan. Never spray liquid into the motor or use the fan outdoors.

Troubleshooting
If the fan pulses on high speed, verify that the USB supply supports 3 A and use a cable shorter than 1.5 m. This fan does not cool the room below ambient temperature and has no thermostat.
