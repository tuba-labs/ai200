Aurora Trail Lamp — fictional product manual, revision A

Specifications
The Aurora Trail Lamp outputs 420 lumens in high mode and 65 lumens in reading mode. Its operating temperature is -10 to 40 degrees Celsius. The sealed battery is 4800 mAh. It weighs 310 grams. The enclosure is IP54 splash resistant, not waterproof for immersion.

Charging
Charge the Aurora Trail Lamp through its USB-C port with a 5 V, 2 A supply. A full charge takes 3 hours. The amber indicator means charging; a steady green indicator means fully charged. Do not charge below 0 degrees Celsius. Unplug the supply before closing the weather cover.

Controls
Press the round button once for reading mode, twice for high mode, and three times to turn the lamp off. Hold the button for 3 seconds to lock the controls for transport. Repeat the 3-second hold to unlock them. A double blink confirms the lock changed.

Runtime and care
Typical runtime is 6 hours in high mode and 28 hours in reading mode. Clean with a damp cloth. Never immerse the lamp or open the sealed battery compartment. There is no Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, or remote-control feature.
